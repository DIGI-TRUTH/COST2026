Governance, management and adaptive coordination

Four Working Groups; explicit delivery functions; one shared assessment and adjustment cycle.

Governance and daily management

The Management Committee decides; the Core Group prepares and steers. Dedicated part-time management support handles daily administration with the Grant Holder. Scientific, training and monitoring functions have explicit responsibilities and can be assigned to existing roles. Young Researchers and Innovators can contribute as mentored assistants or co-leads; career stage alone does not establish management experience.

Six mixed transfer packages organise capacity-building through reciprocal skills learning across the four Working Groups. Package Leads coordinate learning content and mentoring with the training lead.

Recruitment and network development

The training and network-development lead works with WG leaders to identify missing skills and organise targeted open invitations. Communication circulates them through the portal and partner channels. The Management Committee considers participation through applicable COST procedures. Induction and proposed learning roles are logged in the internal progress panel; public matrices remain institutional.

Adjustment triggers

EvidenceProposed response
New members or gapsOpen invitations, induction and proposed package allocation within one cycle.
Inactive pathway or country for two semestersOffer a learning role and review mobility priorities under published criteria.
Single-provider competence after Year 1Prioritise reciprocal learning and train-the-trainer support.
Low package uptakeReview format, level, language and delivery; consider alignment with another pathway, subject to MC decision.
Unavailable leadActivate the agreed backup; MC reviews appointments where required.
Participation imbalanceReview outreach and selection criteria under applicable rules.
Weak learning evidenceRevise teaching format; distinguish perceived usefulness from assessed skill.
Low stakeholder useTest accessibility and usability; adapt dissemination formats and channels.
Self-declared competence without evidenceRecord as self-declared until supporting work or assessment is available.
