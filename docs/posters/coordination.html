Governance and daily management

The Action Chair leads science and management and is the main contact with COST. The Vice-Chair supports and substitutes for the Chair. Four Working Groups have six leaders/co-leaders in total. The Management Committee decides; the Core Group prepares and steers. Dedicated management support handles daily administration with the Grant Holder.

Six mixed transfer packages organise capacity-building through reciprocal skills learning across the four Working Groups. Package Leads coordinate learning content and mentoring with the training lead.

Scientific, training and monitoring functions remain explicit within the agreed structure where feasible.

Grant selection and finance: the Management Committee defines criteria, selection and budgets, directly or through a committee under its approved mandate. The Chair and WG leaders may contribute under that procedure; conflicts of interest are managed and decisions recorded. The Grant Awarding Coordinator coordinates calls, evaluation and approvals on behalf of the MC. Receiving funds, financial administration and payments belong to the Grant Holder and its management arrangements.

Management effort, backups and resources are agreed with the Grant Holder in the management plan, within the applicable Grant Agreement and administration/coordination arrangements. The proposal does not presume a new salaried appointment or a fixed budget allocation.

Recruitment and network development

The training and network-development lead works with WG leaders to identify missing skills and organise targeted open invitations. Communication circulates them through the portal and partner channels. The Management Committee considers participation through applicable COST procedures. Induction and proposed learning roles are logged in the internal progress panel; public matrices remain institutional.
