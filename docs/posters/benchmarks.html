What the comparative cases teach us

Common designs connect claim production, mediation and audience reception. Shared definitions need contextual limits, and public resources improve through documented user feedback.

Evidence gates within the existing plan

M3: confirm capacity and access. M6: define audiences and baselines. M9–12: pilot and feedback. M15: freeze comparative core. M18–21: retest materials. M24: check reliability before pooling. M30: versioned resources and synthesis. M36: publish assessed resources and experiment results. M42: review documented use. M48: record maintenance decisions.

Four Working Groups and six reciprocal transfer pathways remain. No new deliverable IDs, research funding or guaranteed policy uptake are implied. Repertoire compatibility is tested separately from observed acceptance.
